ScrollScore 支援

最後更新:2026 年 10 月

ScrollScore 把樂譜變成橫向捲動的播放畫面,可以跟著播放軸看譜、聽音樂,也能匯出成影片。

如果問題跟某份樂譜有關,你可以選擇附上檔案。檔案只用來重現問題,問題處理完就刪除,不會分享或用在其他地方。

快速開始

  1. 開始畫面點一個範例(小星星、歡樂頌、搖滾鼓、放克鼓),或按「載入樂譜」選自己的檔案。
  2. 按「播放」開始;點樂譜空白處也可以播放/暫停。左右滑動樂譜可以快速跳小節。
  3. 速度、樂器、移調、預備拍等都在「設定」裡。

支援的檔案:MusicXML(.xml、.musicxml、.mxl)、Guitar Pro(.gp、.gpx、.gp5、.gp4、.gp3)。MuseScore、Finale、Sibelius、Dorico 都可以匯出 MusicXML。

匯出影片

  1. 按上方的「匯出影片」(攝影機圖示;螢幕很窄時在「設定」裡)。
  2. 選橫式、直式或方形(直式像紙本樂譜一樣一頁一頁翻、鋼琴不顯示鍵盤,橫式與方形是橫向捲動),需要的話改標題、副標題(例如演出者;樂譜有寫作曲者會先帶入)、浮水印,預覽會跟著變;按「開始匯出」。樂譜位置、背景圖、只匯出某一段在「進階」裡。上次的選擇會記住。
  3. 需要影片封面(YouTube 縮圖)時,在同一頁按「存封面圖」,兩種樣式(雜誌排版、置中卡片)會並排出來,點想要的那張就存下來:用目前的設定加上放大的標題與副標題(不放頻道名稱,YouTube 列表旁邊本來就有),橫式是 1280×720。
  4. 支援的裝置(較新的 iPhone、iPad、電腦)不用等樂曲播完,通常比樂曲長度快很多;較舊的裝置需要的時間等於樂曲長度。過程中請讓畫面保持在最前面,不要切到其他 App 或讓螢幕熄滅。
  5. App 版完成後會自動存到「照片」,也可以按「分享」傳到其他 App;網頁版在 iPhone/iPad 上按「儲存影片」,電腦上會直接下載。

常見問題

沒有聲音

請確認裝置音量。第一次播放時需要載入音色,稍等一下就會有聲音;載入過的音色會存在裝置上,之後很快。App 版在靜音模式下也會出聲。

匯出影片時聽不到聲音

這是正常的。匯出時為了避免喇叭爆音會暫時靜音,影片裡的聲音完全不受影響。

手機橫放時沒有鍵盤

手機橫放時畫面高度很小,為了讓樂譜清楚,只顯示譜面;轉回直式鍵盤就會出現。

樂譜顯示或播放跟原本的不一樣

不同軟體匯出的 MusicXML 寫法差異很大,少數記號可能無法顯示。

我的資料會被上傳嗎?

不會。樂譜與影片都只在你的裝置上處理,不需要帳號,也不收集任何資料。詳見隱私權政策。

Support (English)

ScrollScore turns sheet music into a smooth horizontally scrolling view: follow the playhead, listen, and export a video.

Contact
Questions, suggestions or bug reports: steventsaimusic@gmail.com.
If the problem is with a particular score, you may choose to attach the file. It is used only to reproduce the problem, deleted once the issue is resolved, and never shared or used for anything else.

Getting started

  1. Tap a sample on the start screen (Twinkle, Ode to Joy, Rock drums, Funk drums), or tap “Load Score” to open your own file.
  2. Tap “Play”. Tapping an empty area of the score also plays/pauses; swipe left or right to jump between measures.
  3. Tempo, instrument, transpose, count-in and other options are in “Settings”.

Supported files: MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml, .mxl) and Guitar Pro (.gp, .gpx, .gp5, .gp4, .gp3). MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius and Dorico can all export MusicXML.

Exporting a video

  1. Tap “Export” at the top (camera icon; on very narrow screens it is in “Settings”).
  2. Choose landscape, portrait or square (portrait turns pages like printed music and shows no piano keyboard; landscape and square scroll sideways) and edit the title, subtitle (e.g. the artist; filled in with the composer when the score has one) or watermark if you like — the preview updates as you go — then tap “Start Export”. Score position, background image and exporting only part of the piece are under “More options”. Your choices are remembered.
  3. For a video cover (YouTube thumbnail), tap “Save Cover” on the same page and pick one of the two styles (Magazine or Centered card) shown side by side: it uses your current settings with an enlarged title and subtitle (no channel name — YouTube already shows it next to the thumbnail); landscape is 1280×720.
  4. On supported devices (recent iPhones, iPads and computers) export doesn't wait for the piece to play through and is usually much faster; on older devices it takes as long as the piece. Keep the app in the foreground and the screen on.
  5. In the app, the video is saved to Photos automatically; tap “Share” to send it to another app. On the web, tap “Save Video” on iPhone/iPad; on a computer it downloads.

FAQ

No sound: check the volume. Sounds load the first time you play and are then cached on the device. The app also plays with the silent switch on.

No sound while exporting: speakers are muted during export to avoid crackling; the video's audio is unaffected.

No keyboard in landscape on a phone: the screen is too short, so only the score is shown. Rotate to portrait to see the keyboard.

A score looks or sounds different: MusicXML from different programs varies a lot and a few markings may not be shown.

Is my data uploaded? No. Everything is processed on your device, no account is needed and no data is collected. See the privacy policy.
