ScrollScore 隱私權政策

最後更新:2026 年 10 月

ScrollScore 是一個在你的裝置上顯示與播放樂譜、匯出捲動樂譜影片的工具。我們不需要你註冊帳號,也不收集任何個人資料。

  • 你匯入的檔案(樂譜、背景圖片)只在你的裝置上處理,不會上傳到任何伺服器。
  • 匯出的影片只存在你的裝置上,由你自己決定要不要分享。
  • 設定(語言、主題、速度、游標顏色等)只存在裝置的本機儲存空間。
  • 音色檔第一次使用時會下載並快取在裝置上,之後可以離線使用。
  • 我們沒有使用任何分析、廣告或追蹤工具,也不會販售或分享任何資料。
  • 如果你主動寄信給我們,我們只用你的信箱與信件內容(包括你選擇附上的檔案)來回覆與處理你的問題;附件在問題處理完後刪除,不會分享或用在其他地方。

網頁版會從 GitHub(音色檔)、jsDelivr(程式庫)與 Google Fonts(字型)下載檔案,這些服務可能依它們各自的隱私權政策記錄連線資訊(例如 IP 位址);我們拿不到這些資料。App 版把這些檔案直接包在 App 裡。

如果有任何問題,請寄信到 steventsaimusic@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: October 2026

ScrollScore displays and plays sheet music on your device and exports scrolling-score videos. It requires no account and collects no personal data.

  • Files you import (scores, background images) are processed on your device only and are never uploaded.
  • Exported videos stay on your device; you decide whether to share them.
  • Settings (language, theme, tempo, cursor colour, etc.) are stored locally on your device.
  • Instrument sounds are downloaded and cached on your device the first time they are used, and then work offline.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we never sell or share any data.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message (including any files you choose to attach) only to reply to you and resolve your issue. Attachments are deleted once the issue is resolved and are never shared or used for anything else.

The web version downloads files from GitHub (sounds), jsDelivr (libraries) and Google Fonts (fonts); these services may log connection details such as your IP address under their own privacy policies, and we have no access to that information. The app version includes these files in the app itself.
