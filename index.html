關於與授權›

ScrollScore 使用以下開放授權的音色、資料與程式庫。音色檔經過處理(格式轉換、音量對齊、立體聲與長度調整)。

  • 鋼琴音色 Accurate-Salamander Grand Piano V6.2 (Chisato Yamauchi), based on Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm — CC BY 3.0
  • 鼓組音色 Naked Drums by Wilkinson Audio (SFZ edition by kinwie) — CC BY 4.0
  • 音樂廳殘響 Open Database of Spatial Room Impulse Responses at Detmold University of Music (S. V. Amengual Garí, B. Şahin, D. Eddy, M. Kob) — CC BY 4.0
  • 樂譜顯示 OpenSheetMusicDisplay (BSD‑3‑Clause), VexFlow (MIT)
  • Guitar Pro 匯入 alphaTab (MPL-2.0)
  • 壓縮樂譜(.mxl) JSZip (MIT)
  • 影片封裝 mp4-muxer, webm-muxer (MIT)
  • 演奏參考資料 ASAP dataset (F. Foscarin et al.), MAESTRO and Groove MIDI Dataset (Google Magenta)
